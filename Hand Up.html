Hand Up

Say what you need. Someone who can help will see it.

Hand Up is an open board. People post one specific need, like school fees, a month of groceries or a sewing machine. People with money or time to spare choose a request and help directly.

Giving and receiving safely
  • Everything on this board is public. Don't post ID numbers, bank card numbers or home addresses.
  • Helpers: pay schools, landlords, clinics and shops directly when you can, and ask questions before sending money.
  • Nobody should ever pay a fee to receive help. If someone asks for one, don't send it.
  • Requesters: share your contact details only in the offer thread you trust, and confirm receipt only when help has actually arrived.
